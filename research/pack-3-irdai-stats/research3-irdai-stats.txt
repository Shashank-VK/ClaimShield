--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SOURCE: IRDAI Annual Report 2023-24 (IRDAI (Insurance Products) Regulations, 2024,

        AR 2024-25 (Master Circular on Health Insurance Business,

Q6-A. PED waiting period maximum (AR 2023-24, "Salient features of Health Insurance
  Products", IRDAI (Insurance Products) Regulations, 2024):
  - Maximum waiting period for DISCLOSED PED: brought down from 48 months to 36 months.
  - Maximum waiting period for UNDISCLOSED PED: brought down from 96 months to 60 months.
  - Period for categorising any condition/ailment/injury/disease as PED: reduced from

    (not applicable to claims arising from accident).

QUESTION 11 — Escalation ladder:
        grievance officer → Bima Bharosa → Ombudsman → consumer commission
        (timelines, limits)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SOURCE: IRDAI Annual Reports, Grievances section and Ombudsman section.

Q11-A. Grievance officer / internal escalation (Protection of Policyholders'
  Interests Regulations, 2017; AR 2022-23 II.6; AR 2024-25 II.6.2):
  - IRDAI mandated all insurers to have a grievance redressal policy; designate a
    Grievance Redressal Officer at Head Office/Corporate Office/Principal Office AND
    at every other office; constitute a Policyholder Protection Committee.
  - AR 2024-25: insurers must have a Policyholders' Protection, Grievance Redressal
    and Claims Monitoring Committee (PPGR&CM); a designated officer at every place of
    business; and a proper internal escalation matrix if grievances are not addressed
    to the complainant's satisfaction.
  -> claim text | S1,S3 | Annual Reports 2022-23 & 2024-25 | OFFICIAL | High

Q11-B. Bima Bharosa portal:
  - IRDAI's online grievance filing/tracking portal, integrated with insurers'
    Complaints Management System; IRDAI Grievance Call Centre (IGCC) toll-free
    telephone/email; monitors insurer disposal.
  -> claim text | S1 | Annual Report 2024-25 | OFFICIAL | High

Q11-C. Insurance Ombudsman:
  - Policyholder first approaches insurer; if unresolved/partially resolved, may go to
    Ombudsman. Ombudsman disposes complaint within 3 months; award binding.
  - Award limit ₹50 lakhs (enhanced from ₹30 lakhs effective 9 Nov 2023).
  - Insurer complies within 30 days; penalty ₹5,000/day + interest for failure.
  -> claim text | S1,S2,S3 | Annual Reports 2022-23 to 2024-25 | OFFICIAL | High

Q11-D. Consumer commission:
  - The escalation step beyond the Ombudsman (consumer forum/consumer commission) and
    its timelines/limits are NOT stated in the Annual Report text fetched.
